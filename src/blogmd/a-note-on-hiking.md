# A note on Hiking

Living in California is a privilege in many ways, and having so many beautiful places to hike nearby is one of them.

![A river running through Yosemite Valley beneath granite cliffs and a waterfall](/blog/a-note-on-hiking.jpg "Yosemite National Park")

There are trails along the coast, through redwood forests, around lakes, and across hills and mountains. Some are close enough for a morning hike, while others can become a full weekend trip. There is always somewhere new to explore.

I have gone on a few hikes since moving here, and I love them. I enjoy the walk, the quiet, the views, and the feeling of reaching the end of a trail after putting in the effort. Even when a hike is tiring, I am usually glad I went.

It is easy to live near all of this and keep saying that I will explore it later. Work, classes, and everything else can fill the week quickly. But I do not want my time in California to pass without seeing more of what is around me.

I know how lucky I am to have this opportunity, and I fully intend to make good use of it.

There are many trails I have not visited and places I have not seen yet. I do not have a grand hiking plan or a checklist to finish. I just want to get outside more often, try new trails, and slowly see more of California.

So, a small note to myself: hike more.
